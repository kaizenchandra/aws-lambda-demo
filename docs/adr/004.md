# ADR-004 — Two business bounded contexts

## Context

The reference implements contracted-customer digital order acceptance and fulfillment registration.

## Decision

Ordering owns immutable accepted orders and pricing; Fulfillment owns receipt registration and completion. No fake
payment/inventory services.

## Alternatives Considered

Five simulated contexts; one shared business service.

## Consequences

Credible smaller scope; payment, finite stock, delivery and email require real business contracts before introducing
sagas.
